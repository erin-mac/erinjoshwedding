Getting Around DC

🚇 Metro

DC's Metro system is convenient and affordable. The nearest Metro station to our venue is NoMa-Gallaudet U on the Red Line.

🛴🚲 E-scooter and E-bike rentals

DC offers extensive e-bike and e-scooter rental options, ranging from shared, app-based dockless vehicles (Lime, Veo, Capital Bikeshare) to daily rentals from local shops like Unlimited Biking, E-MILE, and King Electric Bikes.

🚕 Rideshare

Uber and Lyft are readily available throughout DC.
