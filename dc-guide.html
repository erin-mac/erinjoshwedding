Explore Washington, DC

We love DC and we're excited to share some of our favorite spots with you! Here are our recommendations for things to do, places to eat, and sights to see during your visit.

🍽️

Restaurants

Maydan

Incredible Middle Eastern and North African food, great drinks. You'll need a reservation.

📍 1346 Florida Ave NW, Washington, DC 20009

Red Hen

Our neighborhood pasta bar.

📍 1822 1st St NW, Washington, DC 20001

Union Market Food Hall

Tons of options. We like Fishwife! Best poke bowl in town.

📍 1309 5th St NE, Washington, DC 20002

Indigo

Great casual Indian. Hang out on the patio.

📍 243 K St NE, Washington, DC 20002

A. Litteri

One of Josh's favorite places in DC. Great Italian market with good sandwiches. Or buy a whole log of mortadella.

📍 517 Morse St NE, Washington, DC 20002

La' Shukran

One of our new favorites in the Union Market area. Get a reservation in advance (it's worth it!)

📍 417 Morse St NE, Washington, DC 20002

🍸

Bars

St. Vincent

A great wine bar, modeled after an even greater wine bar we love in New Orleans called Bacchanal.

📍 3212 Georgia Ave NW, Washington, DC 20010

All Souls

A great neighborhood classic cocktail bar. Heavy pours.

📍 725 T St NW, Washington, DC 20001

American Ice

Josh and Erin had their first date here in 2018!

📍 917 V St NW, Washington, DC 20001

Death & Co. D.C.

Josh likes the one in New York better but this is pretty, pretty good!

📍 124 Blagden Alley NW, Washington, DC 20001

Golden Age

Cool art deco bar cave in Dupont!

📍 1726 Connecticut Ave NW, Washington, DC 20009

☕

Cafes & Bakeries

Big Bear

Our great neighborhood coffee shop. Hang out and watch some dogs.

📍 1700 1st St NW, Washington, DC 20001

Seylou

Excellent bakery in Blagden Alley. Get a canelé.

📍 926 N St NW, Washington, DC 20001

Yellow

Good coffee, pastries, and sandwiches in Union Market.

📍 1524 Wisconsin Ave NW, Washington, DC 20007

Nino's Bakery

Erin did a lot of research and their almond croissant is the best in town.

📍 1310 L St NW, Washington, DC 20005

Bread Alley

A secret bakery in Union Market. Erin recommends the walnut cranberry sourdough.

📍 418 Morse St NE, Washington, DC 20002
